wow.export

Active Website GitHub

wow.export is the most popular 3D export toolkit for World of Warcraft, allowing you to export models, textures, sounds, maps and much more into common 3D formats.

Originally as an evolution of WoWExportTools by Marlamin, we've gone on to develop wow.export into a foundational tool for modern content-creation within the World of Warcraft community.

{{thumb=static/images/projects/wow.export/screen1.png?width=530&popout&title=Export Characters}} {{thumb=static/images/projects/wow.export/screen2.png?width=530&popout&title=Export Maps}} {{thumb=static/images/projects/wow.export/screen3.png?width=530&popout&title=Model Viewer}} {{thumb=static/images/projects/wow.export/screen4.png?width=530&popout&title=Item Browser}}

wow.export has been used in the creation of projects such as the Oscar-nominated Netflix documentary The Remarkable Life of Ibelin, the official Mythic Dungeon International and Arena World Championship trailers and so many other projects by talented content-creators and makers.

Archaeology - 2023 Mod Contest

1st Place Winner Link

My winning entry for the 2023 Melvor Idle mod contest! Archaeology is a passive skill that adds six themed digsites for players to construct, excavate and master. Each digsite brings unique new items in the form of collectibles, treasure and interactive curiosities.

Each digsite features rare artifacts that come together as a collection, unlocking a unique pet upon completion. In addition to artifacts and treasure, you also have a rare chance to uncover curiosities - items that hold great rewards, though reaching the treasure inside is not always so simple!

{{thumb=static/images/projects/melvor/arch1.png?width=530&popout}} {{thumb=static/images/projects/melvor/arch2.png?width=530&popout}} {{thumb=static/images/projects/melvor/arch3.png?width=530&popout}} {{thumb=static/images/projects/melvor/arch4.png?width=530&popout}}

When designing my contest submission, I wanted to create something that felt like official content - cohesive with the game mechanics while staying thematically grounded in the RuneScape vibe that Melvor Idle is inspired by.

Runsafe (Minecraft Server)

2009 - 2015 GitHub

Back in 2009, I was in a guild raiding Ulduar in World of Warcraft. After raids, more and more of us started trying out this new game called Minecraft. We quickly set up a little server for our guild, which then expanded to friends of friends.

As popularity grew, we began building a framework to help manage it. Things evolved rapidly from there - the server eventually reached over 70,000 players. With a completely bespoke server core, we stood out for our ability to do things differently.

{{thumb=static/images/projects/runsafe/image1.png?width=530&popout}} {{thumb=static/images/projects/runsafe/image2.png?width=530&popout}}

The server still exists today, over a decade later, and is now actively maintained by some of the original moderators.
