Artwork

Between the years of 2015 and 2022, I created hundreds of World of Warcraft 3D renders. This started out as an experimental process to explore what was possible with wow.export as we developed it, but grew into a side hobby and eventually into commissioned work.

Below is a gallery of my favourite pieces in no particular order.
