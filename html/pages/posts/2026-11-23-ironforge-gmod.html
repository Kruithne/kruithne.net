Is this an update about any of the many projects I am working on? Of course it isn't. For reasons known only to the people who will eventually study my brain when it's in a jar, instead of working on any of my current projects, I instead found the unavoidable urge to revisit one from five years ago.

Garry's Mod

Back in 2021 I had this lovely idea of putting Ironforge from World of Warcraft into Garry's Mod. It's was an awful process and the result, while it seemed great at the time, was rather sub-par.

Distractions

Jokes about my inability to stay focused on a project aside, this was not actually the unnecessary tangent that it immediately appears to be. For a while now I've been working on a new version of wow.export, and with the amount of significant changes being made I want to be absolutely sure it works well.

One of the best ways to ensure a product good is to dogfood it. So that's essentially what this was for. It's easy to click random buttons and go "yes this works", but actually having a reason to use the output is the quickest way to validate if the output is garbage or not.
