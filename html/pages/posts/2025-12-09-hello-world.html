You've reached my brand new website! More specifically, you're now reading the first entry in my new "DevLog". What is this? Motivation, hopefully!

For the better part of the last decade, I've wanted to make my own games completely from scratch, and I have actually been doing that. But I've never committed to them, and I always scrap them before they become anything I want to show to the world.

The biggest problem I have is doing too much. I have way too many side projects, and as soon as I start making headway with "the big one", another carrot runs past and I'm off to chase that.

Moving forward, I want to start making posts here. In the beginning, they're going to be mostly technical posts, but as the games progress, it'll evolve into more content-orientated posts. At least that's the goal.

Honestly, I don't know what this will become. I don't even know if anyone will read it. But we'll see what happens! This is sort of a placeholder post for now, so stay tuned for something a bit more... well, more.
